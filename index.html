Finding 2: New quote requests have no single owner
What we saw

Requests arrive by form, phone and email into a shared inbox. In two weeks, 9 of 41 had no reply logged within one business day.

Team and AI

Two staff draft quote emails with a free AI chat tool, with no guidance on customer details.

Recommendation

Log every request with an owner and send a reminder if nobody replies. Agree one approved AI tool and simple rules for customer information.

Priority: High Effort: Small Outcome to agree: a logged reply to every request within one business day

Problem 1 · Leads and quotes

A quote request sits in the inbox and nobody answers it.

BEFORE

AFTER

How I'd tackle it
  • Log every new request in one place, with an owner.
  • Set a follow-up window that fits your business.
  • Remind the owner, not the whole team, when it passes.

Problem 2 · Your team and AI

Everyone is trying AI their own way, and nobody knows what works.

BEFORE
Each person picks a tool Customer details pasted anywhere Results vary by person
AFTER
One plan for the business Approved tools and ground rules Hands-on training Team owns the results
Dark boxes are steps a person still owns.
How I'd tackle it
  • Find out how people already use AI, without judgement.
  • Agree tools, rules for customer information, and where a person checks the output.
  • Train the team on their real work, then follow up.
Problem 3 · Repetitive admin

Reminders go out by hand and the same details get typed twice.

BEFORE
Form, email or schedule Retyped into a spreadsheet Retyped again, or texted one by one Skipped on busy days
AFTER
Form, email or schedule Details copied over once Reminders sent on schedule Office handles replies
Dark boxes are steps a person still owns.
How I'd tackle it
  • Map which details move between which tools.
  • Copy them across in one step and send reminders automatically.
  • Keep a quick human check where a mistake would be costly.
